## The Core Loop: Accept or Decline

The central mechanic, as advertised, is a binary decision. A car appears, and you either take it or pass on it. This is the "collection simulator" format rather than a racing or driving game.

The official description frames accepting and declining as the way you "earn money and expand your collection." It does not spell out the exact trade-offs in detail. Based on the format and on what third-party guides describe, the practical tension usually works like this: taking a car adds it to your collection, while declining may preserve money or a slot for something better. Because the developer has not published the underlying rules, treat any specific claim about which choice is "correct" as a community interpretation, not a documented fact.

The safest approach is to test it yourself early. Play a few offers, watch how your money and collection respond to each decision, and let the game's own feedback tell you what the trade-off is on the current version.
