## The accept-or-decline loop, explained plainly

The description frames the game around a repeated choice. Cars are offered to you; you accept or decline each one. Accepting builds out your collection, and the description ties collection growth to earning money. Declining presumably keeps you from filling limited space with cars you do not want — though the exact consequences of declining are not documented by the developer, so the practical value of a "no" is something you learn by playing.

What the official text does support is the direction of progression: money and collection size feed into unlocking more cars and more floors. That is a vertical loop rather than a horizontal one. You are not wandering a map hunting spawns; you are responding to offers and managing what you keep.

Because the description does not specify how offers are generated or how often they appear, any claim about "best offer timing" or "optimal decline strategy" would be invented. Test it yourself in a live server and watch how your money and collection respond.
