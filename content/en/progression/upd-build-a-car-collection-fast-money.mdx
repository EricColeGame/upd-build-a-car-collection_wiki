## How the core loop actually works

The advertised loop is a single-decision loop, which is what separates this game from driving simulators. You are not racing, tuning, or exploring an open map as the primary activity. A car is offered, you accept or decline, and the outcome affects your money and your collection.

Practical reasoning follows from that design:

- **Accepting** adds a car to your personal collection, which is the visible measure of progress.
- **Declining** is not necessarily a mistake. If the game ties the offer to a cost or a slot, a decline can preserve resources for a better offer later. The official description does not state whether declining has a cost, so verify in your own session before assuming either way.
- **Money** is earned through play and is used to expand the collection, per the description.

Because servers hold up to 8 players, this is a small-session experience rather than a massive multiplayer one. Joining with a few friends is reasonable, but the description does not advertise trading, so do not assume you can swap cars with other players.
