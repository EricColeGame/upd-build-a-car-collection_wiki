## The Core Loop: Accept or Decline

The central mechanic is refreshingly simple, and it is the part most players should internalize first. Cars are offered to you one at a time. You accept or you decline. Accepting adds to your collection; the description ties the overall loop to earning money and expanding that collection.

The practical implication is that this is a decision game, not a driving game in the traditional sense. Your time is spent evaluating offers rather than steering. Because the description does not specify an accept limit, a decline penalty, or a timer, you should not assume any of those exist. The safest approach is to watch the game's own on-screen prompts during your first few offers and let the interface teach you the rules, rather than trusting a third-party summary.

The game supports up to eight players per server. Whether other players can see your collection, trade with you, or compete against you is not documented on the official page, so treat any claim about multiplayer trading or leaderboards as unverified until you confirm it in-game.
