## How the Core Loop Works

The gameplay is built around a single repeated decision. A car is presented to you, and you either accept it or decline it. Accepting typically moves that car into your collection and contributes to your progress; declining passes on it and keeps you moving toward the next offer.

From there, two things grow over time:

**Money.** The official description says you earn money, which is the resource that supports expanding your collection.

**Collection size and quality.** Expanding your collection is the stated goal, and the developer frames the endgame as assembling the rarest collection possible, including rare and expensive supercars.

The progression spine the developer names explicitly is unlocking more cars and more floors of the garage. More floors generally means more space and more options, but the page does not say which floor unlocks what. Anyone who tells you otherwise is working from their own playthrough, not from developer documentation.
