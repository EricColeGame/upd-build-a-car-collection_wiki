## The accept-or-decline loop, explained plainly

Most car games on Roblox ask you to grind currency and then choose a vehicle from a shop. This one inverts that. Cars are presented to you, and your only decision is yes or no. Accepting adds the vehicle to your personal collection; declining moves you along to the next offer.

The strategic tension comes from money. The official description says you earn money and expand your collection, and that you unlock more cars and more floors as you progress. So each decision is really two decisions: does this car improve my collection, and does keeping it leave me in a good position for what comes next? Because the developer does not publish a value list, players learn relative worth through repetition, by comparing what they already own, and by watching what other players in the same eight-person server are collecting.

## Playing well without published numbers

Since Fountain Entertainment has not released a spreadsheet of values, good play comes down to judgment and record-keeping:

1. **Know what you already own.** A duplicate offer is worth less to you than a new entry, and your collection is the thing being scored.
2. **Watch the floors you have access to.** The description ties new floors to unlocking more cars, so garage expansion is a progress signal, not just decoration.
3. **Treat rarity claims skeptically.** "Rare and expensive supercars" is advertised as an outcome you hunt for, not a guaranteed drop.
4. **Use the small server size.** With up to eight players, you can compare collections quickly and get a feel for what other people are holding.
5. **Re-check the official page after updates.** The last update was 2026-09-27, and the description is the developer's own channel for announcing what changed.

None of this guarantees a rare collection. It simply keeps your decisions grounded in what the game actually documents.
