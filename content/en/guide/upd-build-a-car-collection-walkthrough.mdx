## The Core Loop: Accept or Decline

The defining mechanic, per the official description, is the offer. Rather than browsing a shop and buying whatever you want, you are presented with cars and you decide whether to take each one. That decision is the heart of the game.

Practically, this means your progress depends on two habits. Second, awareness of your own goals: if you are chasing supercars, an early offer that does not move you toward that goal may be worth passing on, while a car that fills a gap in your collection may be worth taking.
