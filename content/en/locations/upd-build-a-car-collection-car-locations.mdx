## The Core Loop: Accept or Decline

The design is deliberately simple, and that simplicity is the reason the game fits the keyword so well. Rather than dropping you into a world to drive around and find cars, the experience presents each vehicle as an offer. You look at it, you decide, and you move on.

Two consequences follow from that structure:

- **Declining is not failure.** The official description treats declining as a normal half of the decision, not a penalty. If an offer does not improve your collection, passing on it costs you nothing but time.
- **Money and collection size move in parallel.** Earning money and expanding the collection are described as paired activities, which suggests the economy and the garage are meant to grow together rather than separately.

Because the developer has not published the underlying numbers, the practical approach is to treat early offers as low-stakes and watch how the game reacts when you accept or decline. The interface itself is the documentation.
