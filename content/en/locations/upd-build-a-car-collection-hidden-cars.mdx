## How the Accept-or-Decline Loop Works

The core interaction is the one thing the description states most plainly: cars are offered to you, and you decide whether to take each one. Accepting a car adds it to your collection and moves you forward; declining passes on it. Money earned through play feeds back into expanding the collection and unlocking further cars and additional garage floors.

## Collecting Rare Supercars Without Guessing

"Find rare and expensive supercars" and "build the rarest car collection of them all" are aspirational goals from the description, not a checklist. There is no published rarity table, so the practical approach is empirical: play consistently, pay attention to which cars you are offered over time, and keep your collection organized enough that you notice gaps.

A few habits that fit the confirmed loop:

- **Don't auto-accept everything blindly.** The description explicitly frames accepting and declining as choices, which implies the decision carries weight.
- **Track what you decline.** If you are hunting a specific tier of car, knowing what you have passed on helps you judge whether offers are trending in a useful direction.
- **Watch the update banner.** The developer advertises new content through the description, so the official page is where additions like new cars or prestige tiers get announced first.

None of this guarantees a rare pull. It is a collection simulator; the odds are part of the design, and they are not published.
