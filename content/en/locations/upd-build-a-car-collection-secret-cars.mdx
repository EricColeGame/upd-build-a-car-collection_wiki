## The Core Loop: Accept or Decline

The central mechanic is unusually easy to understand. Cars are presented to you, and you decide whether to take each one. Accept and the car joins your collection; decline and you move on. Because the game is built around that single decision, the practical skill is not driving or tuning but judgment — knowing when a car is worth keeping relative to what it does for your money and your garage.

The advertised reward structure reinforces this. Earning money and expanding your collection are described as linked outcomes of the same loop, and unlocking more cars and more floors follows from that expansion. In other words, your collection is not just a trophy shelf; it appears to be the progression track itself. Treat each offer as a step toward the next unlock rather than an isolated collectible.
