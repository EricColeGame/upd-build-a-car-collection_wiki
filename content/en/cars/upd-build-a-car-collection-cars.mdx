## What the game is, according to its official page

## The core loop: accept or decline

The single most important mechanic described by the developer is the choice. Cars are offered to you, and you decide whether to accept or decline each one. That framing matters because it tells you the game is not primarily about driving, racing, or tuning. It is about decisions and accumulation.

Practical reasoning follows from that: if every offer is a yes-or-no decision, then the value of your collection depends on how you weigh each offer against what you already own and what you are trying to unlock. The official description links accepting cars to earning money and expanding your collection, which suggests the two are connected rather than separate systems. It does not explain the exact relationship, so the honest guidance is to watch what changes in your money total and your collection list after each decision during your first few sessions.
