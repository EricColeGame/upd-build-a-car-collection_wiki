## How the Accept-or-Decline Loop Works

The core interaction is a decision, not a minigame. A car appears, and you either accept it into your collection or decline it. Accepting builds your collection and, based on the advertised features, contributes to earning money. Declining keeps your options open for the next offer.

Practical reasoning follows from that structure. Because the game's stated progression is tied to expanding your collection and unlocking more cars and floors, the accept-or-decline choice is the main lever you control. A few habits tend to serve players well in this kind of simulator:

- Pay attention to what the game actually rewards, rather than what a thumbnail claims. If accepting a car increases money or progress, that is visible on screen.
- Do not assume a "rare" label from a third party. Rarity in this game is tied to the game's own categories, and diamond cars are a developer-advertised tier, not a fan invention.
- Treat your garage as the progression track. Unlocking more floors and more cars is described as the reward for expanding your collection, so a thin collection limits what you can reach.
