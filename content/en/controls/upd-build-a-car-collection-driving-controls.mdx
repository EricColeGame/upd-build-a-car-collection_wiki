## The Core Loop: Accept or Decline

The advertised mechanic is deliberately minimal. Cars are presented to you, and you make a binary choice.

Accepting a car adds it to your collection. Declining passes on it. The official description ties accepting and declining to earning money and expanding your collection, which means the decision is not purely cosmetic — it feeds the economy that unlocks more cars and more floors of your garage.

Because the page does not document the exact consequences of either choice, treat any specific claim about "always accept" or "always decline" strategies as a third-party observation rather than developer-confirmed information. The safest way to learn the tradeoffs is to watch how your money and collection change across several offers in a single session.
