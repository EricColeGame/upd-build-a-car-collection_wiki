## How the Accept-or-Decline Loop Works

The mechanic that defines this game is choice under uncertainty. Rather than driving to a dealership and browsing a fixed catalog, you are presented with cars one at a time and you respond to each offer. Accepting adds to your collection and, based on the advertised features, feeds into earning money. Declining passes on that offer and presumably moves you to the next one.
