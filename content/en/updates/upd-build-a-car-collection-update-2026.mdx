## FAQ

**Is the upd build a car collection update 2026 officially confirmed?**
Treat anything you see before official patch notes as community reports rather than confirmed details. Developers typically publish release notes alongside the build, and that is the moment to trust specific numbers, prices, and dates. Until then, prepare generally rather than chasing rumors.

**Should I buy new cars right after the update launches?**
No. Launch pricing is almost always inflated by hype. Play the new content with your existing collection, watch how the market behaves for a week or two, then buy once values settle — unless the car is limited to the event window.

**Will my existing cars lose value in the 2026 update?**
Some will, especially mid-tier cars that overlap with new additions. Limited and time-gated vehicles tend to hold value better because supply is fixed. This is exactly why a flexible garage and a currency buffer matter more than owning one expensive car.

**How do I keep up with official information?**
Follow the developer's official channels, read the full patch notes rather than summaries, and check in-game announcements. Player experience is helpful for strategy, but official notes are the only reliable source for what actually changed.
