## The core loop: accept, decline, repeat

The accept-or-decline decision is the heart of the game. Instead of browsing a shop and buying whatever you want, you are shown a car and must decide on the spot. This single mechanic shapes everything else:

- Acceptance adds the car to your collection and, per the description, ties into earning money.
- Declining passes on that offer and presumably moves you to the next one.
- Money lets you expand your collection, which in turn unlocks more cars and additional garage floors.

What the official page does not explain is the exact order of operations. It does not say whether declining carries a penalty, whether money arrives only through accepted cars, or how many cars appear per round. Those details exist only inside the game itself. If you want precision, the reliable method is to play a session and watch the interface — the game tells you far more than any listing does.
