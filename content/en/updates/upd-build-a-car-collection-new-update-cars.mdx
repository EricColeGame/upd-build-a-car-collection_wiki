## The Core Loop: Accept or Decline

The official description is short and specific about the main interaction. Cars are offered to the player, and the player either accepts or declines each one. That single decision drives everything else the description advertises:

- earning money
- expanding a personal collection
- unlocking more cars
- unlocking more floors of the garage
- finding rare and expensive supercars

Read those points together and the intended loop becomes clear. Accepting a car is not just a cosmetic choice; it feeds the collection and the money pool, which in turn opens up more offers and more garage space. Declining exists because not every car is worth a slot — but the official description does not explain exactly how declining affects your money, your offer pool, or your odds of seeing something better later.
